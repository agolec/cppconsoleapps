Using the Modulus operator and integer division

The modulus operator is pretty useful. If you need the rightmost digit of a number, you can
use % and divide by 10, then the rightmost number will be the remainder.

Because any number divided by 10 will decrease it by one magnitude, leaving that rightmost number behind.

2026 % 10 = (202 x 10) + 6

	we would get 6 back from modulus operation 2026 % 10.
